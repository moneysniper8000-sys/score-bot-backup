Privacy Policy

Score Bot Backup is a personal utility used only by its owner to back up application database files to Google Drive.

Data Access

The application accesses Google Drive only for the purpose of creating, reading, updating, and deleting backup files created by the application.

Data Sharing

No personal data is sold, shared, or provided to third parties.

Data Retention

Backup files remain in the owner's Google Drive until they are deleted by the owner or automatically removed as part of the backup retention process.

Contact

This application is maintained for personal use only.
